## 改动说明 / Modifications

本仓库的动画素材是**改编**，不是原始文件：按角色包围盒裁剪（640×360 → 380×336）、缩放至 320×283、
重编码为 VP9 + alpha、部分动作改名、导出 `poster.webp` 首帧。因此同样受 CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 约束。

The clips here are **adapted**, not original: cropped to the character bounding box
(640×360 → 380×336), scaled to 320×283, re-encoded as VP9 with alpha, some renamed, and the first
frame exported as `poster.webp`. They are therefore bound by the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms.
